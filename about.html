Why write in the margins?

This is an anonymous journal of essays about artificial intelligence, ethics, technology, and the world as it becomes.

The machine age is not a separate world. It is this world, under modification.

Most commentary on this topic comes with baggage. This site has none of that. There is no byline because there is no brand to serve.

What to expect

Essays, not hot takes. Written slowly, revised often, published when they're ready.

Topics: AI, Ethics, Technology & society, The world.

House rules

No hype. No jargon deployed to impress. Every claim that matters gets a reason attached.
