Why write in the margins?

This is an anonymous journal of essays about artificial intelligence, ethics, technology, and the world as it becomes.

Most commentary on this topic comes with baggage: a company to market, a book to sell, a reputation to protect, a career to build. This site has none of that. There is no byline because there is no brand to serve.

Essays, not hot takes. Written slowly, revised often, published when they're ready.

Topics: AI, Ethics, Technology, society, and the human things that no model can capture.

Marginal Note — anonymous, independent, unhurried
